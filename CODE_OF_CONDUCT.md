# Code of Conduct

LiveMerge follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). This is a short summary; the
Covenant is the full reference.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and kind, and assume good intentions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, trolling, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this
code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they consider harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile,
[@HenriquePassarelli](https://github.com/HenriquePassarelli). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, and discussions.
